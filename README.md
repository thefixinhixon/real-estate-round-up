# The Real Estate Roundup — Homepage Prototype

Prototype homepage for **The Real Estate Roundup** (Helpful Publications — 26 years in business), a monthly real estate magazine with two editions:

- **The Real Estate Roundup — Lincoln County Edition** — Ruidoso, Alto, Capitan, Carrizozo, Hondo, Glencoe, San Patricio, Lincoln
- **The Real Estate Roundup — Otero County Edition** — Alamogordo, Cloudcroft, Tularosa, High Rolls, La Luz, Mountain Park, Mayhill, Timberon, Weed

## Brand direction

**Modern Mountain** (selected 2026-10-13):
Pine `#123528` · Forest `#1E5A41` · Brass `#D9A441` · Sand `#E8DDD0` · Rust `#B4552D`
Bold grotesque wordmark (Archivo), full-bleed mountain photography, price-first listing cards.
Lincoln County Edition leans pine + brass; Otero County Edition leans rust + sand.

## Naming and contact rules (Susan Hixon, 2026-10-13)

- The title is **never hyphenated** anywhere it appears: always "The Real Estate Roundup".
- The business does **not** take calls or correspondence from the viewing public. Viewers contact the listing agents or companies directly.
- The advertising phone number appears **only** behind the "Advertise in The Real Estate Roundup" link on the site. It is not listed in this README.
- The retired phone number and PO Box from old listings must not be republished.

## Prototype notes

- This is a **design prototype**. The featured listings are clearly labeled samples in a realistic price range — they are **not** live listings.
- Live listings will be generated from the magazine's original Adobe InDesign files (structured extraction for SEO), not from PDFs.
