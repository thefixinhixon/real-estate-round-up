About this property

2 bdrm, 2½ bath tucked in the tall pines and backing to National Forest. Though originally built over 50 years ago, it has been extensively updated with new windows, appliances, plumbing, a new bath, and fresh drywall while preserving its cozy mountain feel. Upstairs living area features vaulted ceilings, a fireplace, an open kitchen, a half bath, and a hidden loft, an inviting space for gathering. Step onto either deck to blend into the forest and enjoy birds, squirrels, and the occasional deer. Peaceful and quiet, it’s an ideal retreat for part‑time or full‑time living.
