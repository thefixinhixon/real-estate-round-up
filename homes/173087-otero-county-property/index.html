About this property

Welcome to YOLO, a secluded mountain retreat deep in Lincoln National Forest. This 1788 sq ft cabin on 8.77 acres is bordered by National Forest, offering complete privacy at the end of a winding quarter‑mile driveway. Elk roam the meadow, hummingbirds gather & the Rio Penasco River flows through the property, creating a serene setting. Cabin includes 3 bdrms & 3 baths w/cozy wood‑burning stove. Outdoor living features 2,300 sq ft of deck space plus a 400 sq ft fire‑pit area & maturing willow trees along the river. Practical amenities include detached shop w/garage, attached garage, electricity w/supplemental solar, 2 large water tanks, owned 1000‑gallon propane tank & Starlink® internet (equipment not included). Adjoining 10‑acre parcel is available for $250,000 with discount for purchasing both.
