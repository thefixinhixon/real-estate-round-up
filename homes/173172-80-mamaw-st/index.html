About this property

Escape to the mountains with this spacious 4-bedroom, 2.5-bath home situated on just over 6 acres and backing directly to the Lincoln National Forest. Surrounded by mountain scenery, wildlife, and abundant birdlife, this property offers privacy, space, and the mountain lifestyle you’ve been looking for. Inside, you’ll find vaulted ceilings, a roomy kitchen, and a cozy wood stove that adds warmth and mountain charm. The primary bedroom is conveniently located on the main floor and features an ensuite bath, while upstairs you’ll find three additional bedrooms and a shared full bathroom, providing plenty of room for family and guests. Step outside to the wraparound covered porch, where the porch swing provides the perfect spot to relax, enjoy the peaceful surroundings, watch the mountain wildlife and birds, or simply breathe in the cool mountain air. Plenty of space for outside entertaining! The property features a private well and a large detached 2-car garage with power and another large workshop space, offering plenty of room for vehicles, tools, hobbies, and recreational gear. The owners have also started finishing an additional room at the rear of the garage, with space for a small kitchen area and bathroom. Once completed, this area could offer a variety of possibilities, including guest quarters, a studio, hobby space, or additional living area. With over 6 acres adjoining the Lincoln National Forest, you’ll have an incredible natural playground right outside your door. Enjoy the privacy, explore the surrounding forest, watch the wildlife wander through, or spend your evenings relaxing on the porch under the mountain skies. Bring the family and start enjoying the cool mountain air! Whether you’re searching for a full-time mountain home, weekend getaway, or a property with plenty of room to spread out and make your own, 80 Mamaw offers acreage, privacy, National Forest access, and endless possibilities in the beautiful High Nogal area.
