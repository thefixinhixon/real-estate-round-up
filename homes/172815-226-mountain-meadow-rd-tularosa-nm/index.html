About this property

exceptional 10+ acre property offering breathtaking 360-degree views of the surrounding mountains and the iconic White Sands. This move-in ready Southwest-style home is fully fenced and designed for those seeking space, versatility, and rural living at its finest. The property features a metal shop with power, water, a half bath, and an attached greenhouse, offering the potential to be converted into a casita, guest quarters, studio, or hobby space. With stunning views in every direction, extensive improvements, and endless possibilities, this unique property is ready for its next owner to make it their own.
