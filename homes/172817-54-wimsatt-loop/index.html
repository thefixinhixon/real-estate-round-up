About this property

Turn-Key Mountain Retreat w/Proven Airbnb Income - Established 5-star short-term rental with strong income history. Being sold mostly furnished and ready to continue operating from day one. Located just 7 miles from Cloudcroft in the Sacramento Mountains, this spacious 5-bedroom, 3-bath home sits on a private 0.89-acre lot offering mountain views, privacy, and year-round wildlife sightings. Designed for comfort and gathering, the home features two living areas, a large kitchen and dining space, and a flexible bonus room ideal for additional sleeping space, office use, or recreation. A cozy pellet stove creates a warm mountain atmosphere, while large windows throughout bring in natural light and scenic forest views. The layout includes five generously sized bedrooms and three full bathrooms, making it ideal for large groups, vacation use, or continued short-term rental operation. Outdoor living is highlighted by a covered deck perfect for enjoying the fresh mountain air, elk and deer sightings, and peaceful surroundings. The nearly one-acre lot provides space, privacy, and a true mountain retreat feel. Conveniently located near Cloudcroft’s shops, dining, skiing, hiking, and outdoor recreation, the property offers both seclusion and accessibility. Whether you’re looking for a primary residence, vacation home, or income-producing investment, this property delivers flexibility with a proven rental history already in place.
